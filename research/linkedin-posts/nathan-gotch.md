## Post 6 — 6 May 2026 — 47 likes, 16 comments — HIGH SIGNAL (workflow detail)

URL: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nathangotch_when-creating-pages-for-seo-aeo-they-activity-7457790873058291712-1MuE

When creating pages for SEO / AEO...

They both work when you have the right ingredients.

If you slop it up, then your results will be temporary.

Some ways to elevate you AI content:

1. Build your search brainiac
- Data layer: GSC, GA4, AI search / rank tracking (Rankability)
- Company layer: products/services, FAQ, etc.
- Expertise / experience layer: founder / SME interviews, UGC, etc.

2. Create swipe file
- Study your top competitors
- Convert their best content into a template
- Repeat for ~100 assets

3. Research the right way
- Identify top competitors for your target keyword from traditional search + AI citations
- Extract entities + content structure

4. Perform a page-level gap analysis
- Throw the top competitors into ChatGPT and ask it to identify topic gaps

5. Build your purple cow content strategy
- You're equipped with what works, what's relevant, and the gaps. Now create a content angle.

6. Create a killer content brief

It should include:

- intelligence from your search brainiac
- least 3 proven template that matches the content type
- most important entities to cover
- purple cow differentiation

Take those steps and you'll build a content outline that easily differentiates you from the competitors.

Now whether you use AI or human to finish it out won't matter.

Good luck out there search pros

P.S. Rankability automates this entire process in less than 5 minutes.

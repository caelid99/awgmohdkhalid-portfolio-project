## Key claims and takeaways

- **Six-step system:** build a keyword template → pick one dedicated category/cluster → source keyword ideas → filter the set with metrics → deep-dive individual keywords → prioritise into a 30–90 day execution sprint. Blanket keyword research across the whole site is explicitly rejected.
- **Five stages of customer awareness** (most aware → product aware → solution aware → problem aware → unaware) drives priority order. Start at the *bottom* of the funnel, not the top; chasing broad high-volume "unaware" topics first is called the classic mistake. The `best + [solution] (+ location)` pattern is where "most of the magic happens" and is the query type he tracks in both traditional search and AI answers.
- **Search volume is demoted to one variable among many.** Demand can equally be proven by Google Search Console impressions, engagement on Reddit/Quora, or first-party sources such as sales calls and coaching-call questions. An 80/20 split is prescribed: 80% of topics with proven demand, 20% reserved as experimental zero-volume bets.
- **CPC from Google Ads is used as a commercial-intent proxy, not an ad metric.** Advertisers spending on a topic signals commercial value; the *absence* of advertisers is treated as a warning that the topic doesn't convert, not as a blue-ocean opportunity.
- **Competition is assessed at three levels — domain, page, and brand.** The brand level is the AI-era addition: brands that underperform in traditional rankings still get recommended repeatedly in AI answers because of reviews, consensus, and off-site signals. Content quality of incumbents is folded into the budget estimate: "you are not going to be able to come in with some AI slop and beat them."
- **Position drives prioritisation via three automatic buckets:** positions 2–15 are low-hanging fruit and always take priority; position 50+ becomes a clustering opportunity requiring a new page; no ranking at all is "untapped." Moving a page from 5 to 2 is framed as a dramatic visibility gain versus building new.
- **One core topic per page**, placed in the URL, meta description, title tag, H1, and first sentence, with variants used through the body. Keywords are classified as primary, keyword variation (same page), or secondary — a secondary keyword ranking poorly (his example: position 67 on a too-broad page) is the signal to splinter off a dedicated, more specific page.
- **Free sourcing stack, in order:** Google Search Console first (best data for local queries, which most tools miss), Google Ads Keyword Planner geo-filtered to the target city, Reddit, Google's People Also Ask (expanded repeatedly), Perplexity, and Claude loaded with the "SEO super intelligence" knowledge base for first-party topic ideas.
- **Volume of work is deliberately capped.** A raw database of 500–1,000 keywords is filtered to roughly 100, then to 25–50 unique-intent keywords per cluster (10–20 for a focused sprint), because targeting 500 keywords "creates tons of paralysis" and low-quality work. Sprints exist partly to give teams a sense of completion instead of a perpetual SEO loop.
- **Localisation rule:** only add a city modifier where the answer genuinely varies by location. "How much does an SEO consultant cost in St. Louis" is valid; "what is creatine in St. Louis" is called a wasteful, manipulative query.
- Scoring in the template combines volume, keyword difficulty, CPC, position, intent, manual relevance, target word count, lowest competitor domain score, and count of SERP features (AI Overview, ads, People Also Ask, local pack) as an organic-CTR drag. Gotch positions Rankability as the automation of this whole process (~5 minutes versus the manual build) and offers a free 97-point AI SEO checklist at rankability.com/go/checklist.

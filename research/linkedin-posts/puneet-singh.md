Google just launched AI traffic reporting in GA4...

...and Google's own Gemini is already breaking it. 🤦‍♂️

The new AI Assistant channel was supposed to group chatbot traffic automatically.

Instead, many marketers are seeing:

❌ ChatGPT traffic showing as "Unassigned"

❌ Claude traffic showing as "Referral"

❌ Gemini traffic not consistently landing in Google's own AI bucket

The reason?

GA4 still relies on referral data that isn't always passed correctly by browsers and apps.

And when users click through from ChatGPT or Gemini mobile apps, that traffic can end up looking like Direct traffic anyway.

The result:

Your AI traffic numbers may be far less accurate than GA4 suggests.

So before you delete your custom channel groups and regex rules...

double-check the data first.

Google can't even get Gemini to consistently classify inside Google's own analytics platform yet.

Anyone else seeing strange AI traffic numbers in GA4?

If someone's selling you GEO that doesn't look like normal SEO with fresh thinking around agents, you're being sold a story.

URL: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/puneet-singh0_we-tracked-1885-pages-adding-schema-ai-activity-7459812664685285376-ogYj

🚨 "Just add schema for more AI citations." 🚨

That might officially be the biggest SEO myth of 2026.

Ahrefs tracked 1,885 pages adding JSON-LD schema across:
- Google AI Overviews
- Google AI Mode
- ChatGPT

Result?

Basically… nothing.

No meaningful uplift in AI citations.

In fact, AI Overview citations actually dropped slightly.

Here's the part most people will completely miss though:

EVERY page in the study already had 100+ AI Overview citations BEFORE schema was added.

Meaning these pages were already:
✅ crawled
✅ indexed
✅ understood
✅ inside the AI consideration set

So this study does NOT prove schema is useless.

It proves schema alone probably won't increase AI visibility for pages AI systems already trust.

That's a massive distinction.

Because for pages with:
- weak entity understanding
- poor crawlability
- limited indexing
- no AI visibility yet

…schema could still absolutely matter.

The study even admits it can't measure that.

The real takeaway?

AI search visibility still looks suspiciously like regular SEO:

→ authority
→ mentions
→ links
→ topical depth
→ freshness
→ brand trust
→ distribution

Not "add FAQ schema and win ChatGPT."

A lot of "AI SEO frameworks" are about to age very badly.
